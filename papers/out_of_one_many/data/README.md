`anesgpt3_task3.csv` is file 6711747 of the replication package for Argyle et al. (2023), Harvard Dataverse
doi:10.7910/DVN/JPV20K (CC0 1.0): 4,270 respondents of the 2016 ANES Time Series with the twelve items used in
Study 3 (ANES variable names as columns) and GPT-3's answer for each item as the target (`*_gpt3` columns).
`paper_cramersv_appendix.json` holds Appendix Tables 11–13 of the paper (Cramer's V, ANES vs GPT-3, 55 pairs);
`paper_table1_2016.json` holds the 2016 columns of Table 1. The raw ANES data require registration at
electionstudies.org and are not redistributed here; everything needed comes from the CC0 package.
